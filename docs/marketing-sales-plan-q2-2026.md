## 4. Positioning & Core Messaging

### 4.1 One-line positioning

*"UAE cybersecurity delivered by certified practitioners — transparent pricing, hands-on work, no consultancy overhead."*

### 4.2 Proof pillars (the three things every asset must reinforce)

1. **Certified practitioners:** CPTS, OSCP, ISO 27001 LA, Azure Security, CDSA — named, not implied.
2. **Implementation, not advice:** we build, configure, test, train, and ship — not just recommend.
3. **Transparent pricing:** published price ranges; no mystery quotes; no hidden scope creep.

### 4.3 Pain-first messaging matrix

| Pain | Our opener |
|---|---|
| "We got a letter from a client asking for ISO 27001" | "We implement ISO 27001 end-to-end in 4 – 6 months, partner with accredited certifiers, and price it up-front." |
| "PDPL is making our lawyers nervous" | "We build a PDPL-ready privacy program in 4 – 6 weeks — policies, DPIA, data map, DSR workflow — fixed scope." |
| "Our audit found 80 cloud config issues" | "We run a CIS-benchmarked cloud assessment and fix the P1s with you, not just hand you a PDF." |
| "Our users keep clicking phishing links" | "We run quarterly phishing sims + targeted training and show you the click-rate trend line." |
| "We want a pen test but the last one was a vuln-scan PDF" | "Our pen tests are OSCP / CPTS-led, manual, and end with a remediation walkthrough — not a Nessus export." |

### 4.4 Messaging anti-patterns (forbidden)

- "World-class," "cutting-edge," "industry-leading," "next-generation"
- Implied decades of company history
- Unverifiable statistics ("we've stopped 1,000,000 attacks")
- Client logos we don't have
- Competitor shaming by name

### 6.1 Pipeline targets

### 6.2 Lead sources & mix (target %)

| Source | Month 1 | Month 4 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Founder / Guna direct outbound | 50% | 25% |
| Website / inbound (SEO + content) | 10% | 35% |
| LinkedIn (founder visibility) | 15% | 20% |
| Referral / partnership | 10% | 15% |
| Events / community | 15% | 5% |

The shift from outbound-heavy to inbound-balanced is the core engine-build for this window.

### 6.3 Sales process (gated stages in CRM)

1. **Lead captured** → auto-enriched (domain, headcount, industry)
2. **Scoping call booked** (30 min, free) → SLA: within 24 business hours
3. **Scoping call completed** → qualification (budget, authority, need, timeline) + capture pain
4. **Proposal sent** → template per service, always includes price range, assumptions, exclusions
5. **Proposal reviewed** → follow-up within 3 business days
6. **Negotiation** → scope and price adjustments; no more than 1 round of discount (cap 10%)
7. **Contract signed** → milestone invoicing; deposit invoice raised the same day
8. **Kick-off scheduled** → within 5 business days of signature

### 6.4 Pricing discipline

- Published ranges are public on every service page (this is a differentiator — do not dilute it).
- **Internal floor prices** (below requires Manoj override). Covers all 15 Year-1 flagship services:

| Service | Floor (AED) |
|---|---:|
| ISO 27001 Implementation & Certification Support | 50,000 |
| ISO 27001 Gap Assessment | 20,000 |
| NESA / UAE IA V2 Gap Assessment | 20,000 |
| UAE PDPL Compliance Advisory | 15,000 |
| Risk Assessment & Risk Register Build | 20,000 |
| Network Penetration Testing | 20,000 |
| Web Application Penetration Testing | 15,000 |
| Vulnerability Assessment (VA only) | 5,000 |
| Phishing Simulation | 8,000 |
| Azure Cloud Security Assessment | 15,000 |
| Microsoft 365 Security Review | 10,000 |
| Firewall & Network Security Review | 12,000 |
| Security Architecture Review | 15,000 |
| Security Awareness Training — Workshops | 8,000 |
| Tabletop Incident Response Exercise | 8,000 |

- Deposit: 50% for engagements > 30,000 AED; 30% for engagements > 100,000 AED.
- Net terms: Net-30 default; Net-60 only for ISO-certified clients or government with executed PO.
- No free pilots. "Free" is the 30-minute scoping call.
- No discounts beyond a single 10% negotiation concession.

### 6.5 Sales collateral to produce in Month 1

- Service one-pagers (PDF) for all Year-1 services — 10 docs
- 3 case-study templates (ready to fill after first 3 engagements)
- Proposal templates per service
- MSA (Master Services Agreement) with UAE legal review
- NDA template (mutual)
- Scoping questionnaire (ISO 27001, VAPT, PDPL, Cloud) — Google Form or Formbricks
- Pricing guide (internal)
- Objection-handling playbook (internal)

### 6.6 Outbound playbook (Guna-led)

**Targets per week (Months 1 – 2):** 50 cold touches / week split across LinkedIn (30), email (15), warm-intro (5).

**Messaging rules**
- Never send a generic pitch
- Every message cites (a) a regulatory trigger relevant to their industry, or (b) a public signal (job posting, breach disclosure, site issue we noticed)
- First message: 80 – 120 words, 1 ask (15-min call)
- Follow-up 1: Day +4, new angle
- Follow-up 2: Day +10, case or resource share
- Stop after 3 attempts; move to nurture list

### 6.7 Inbound handling

- 24-hour response SLA on any contact-form submission (visible on website)
- Immediate auto-reply (Stalwart + admin dashboard) confirming receipt and scoping-call link
- Discovery call within 48 hours whenever possible
- Every lead logged in CRM within 1 business hour

### 6.8 Retention & expansion (within-window)

Even pre-first-client, the engagement-close motion needs an expansion plan:
- Every engagement closes with a 30-minute post-delivery debrief
- Every engagement offers a follow-on retainer (vCISO, MVM, annual awareness)
- Every client is offered the software channel (where scope allows)
- 90-day NPS survey scheduled at signing

## 10. Risks & Circuit-Breakers (4-month window)

| Risk | Early signal | Circuit breaker |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound is too slow to matter by Month 3 | < 300 website uniques/mo by end Month 2 | Double down on outbound; cut Google Ads if not converting; re-target keywords |
| No partnerships signed by Month 2 | 0 partnerships at end of Month 1 | Manoj personally drives partner outreach for 2 weeks; widen candidate pool |
| Proposals stuck ("ghosting") | > 3 proposals unresponded for > 10 days | Call instead of email; offer limited-time scoping session; revise pricing structure |
| Founder-capacity choke point (Manoj in every call) | Calendar saturation; response SLA slipping | Guna runs discovery qualification; only qualified opportunities escalate to Manoj |
| Under-delivery on first client | Scope creep, missed milestone | Hard stop; scope amendment in writing; Manoj personally reviews every deliverable |
| Burn on paid media without returns | LinkedIn CPA > 1,500 AED, Google CPC > 25 AED w/o conversion | Pause channel; reallocate to content + events |
| Marketing quality drop due to speed | Typos, inaccurate claims in published posts | Two-person review (author + Prathima) required before publish; weekly content-QA pass |
| Competitor pressure (copycat positioning) | Similar messaging from local competitor | Lean harder into transparency + publishable technical work (competitors will not keep up) |

## 11. Success Definition at 15 August 2026

We will consider this 4-month plan a success if, by 15 August 2026, we have achieved **at least four of the following five**:

1. ≥ 3 closed-won paying engagements, ≥ 130,000 AED in signed contract value.
2. ≥ 15 active opportunities in pipeline (valued ≥ 500,000 AED cumulative pipeline).
3. ≥ 2 signed referral / introducer partnerships.
4. ≥ 2,000 monthly website uniques; top-10 UAE ranking on ≥ 3 priority keywords.
5. ≥ 10 published blog posts + ≥ 2 public speaking / panel appearances.

If we achieve all five, Q3 – Q4 acceleration is justified (potential Junior Pentester hire pulled forward).
If we achieve fewer than three, a **strategy reset** is triggered — specifically a review of positioning, pricing, and channel mix before committing the remaining marketing budget.
