## 1. Executive Summary

Underwings Cybersecurity Solutions is a UAE-based cybersecurity services company launching in 2026. We deliver hands-on offensive security, cybersecurity governance-risk-compliance (GRC) implementation, human-risk services, and a curated software channel to small and mid-market businesses in the UAE — with a roadmap to expand into the wider GCC by 2028 and selectively into global EU-linked and enterprise segments thereafter.

We are not a consultancy. We are a cybersecurity services firm: our engagements end in tested systems, issued policies, trained people, and working compliance programs — not slide decks.

**Why now**

The UAE is entering the enforcement phase of a generational regulatory shift: the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), NESA / UAE IA V2, ADHICS for healthcare in Abu Dhabi, Dubai ISR v2, and sector-specific CBUAE and DFSA requirements are driving mandatory cybersecurity investment across every sector. SMB and mid-market companies — which make up the overwhelming majority of UAE firms — are underserved by the large consultancy firms (price and scope mismatch) and underserved by pure-play pentest boutiques (no GRC or program delivery). Underwings is built for exactly that gap.

**Why us**

Our five founders collectively hold CPTS, OSCP, CDSA, Security+, CCNP, Fortinet, Azure Security, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor, and GRC Master Course credentials. We deliver implementation, not advice; we price transparently; and we partner with accredited certification bodies rather than marketing ourselves as one. For our 300,000 AED bootstrap we do not need a marketing agency, a sales floor, or a VC runway — we need 8 to 12 engaged clients in Year 1 and we have priced, scoped, and road-mapped the business to reach that target.

## 3. Market Opportunity

### 3.1 Macro drivers in the UAE

- **Regulatory enforcement ramp.** UAE PDPL (effective since 2023, enforcement ramping in 2025 – 2026), NESA / UAE IA V2, ADHICS for Abu Dhabi healthcare providers, Dubai ISR v2 for Dubai Government entities and suppliers, CBUAE cybersecurity guidelines for banks, and sector-specific DFSA and ADGM requirements are moving from "paper compliance" to active audit and penalty phase.
- **Digital transformation momentum.** The UAE's cloud-first government strategy, AI adoption across ministries, and the ongoing Smart City / Smart Dubai programs are creating a continuous demand for security testing, cloud posture assessments, and governance uplift.
- **SME regulatory shock.** Unlike enterprises, SMEs in the UAE are typically encountering cybersecurity regulation as a compliance surprise rather than a planned program — driving demand for fast, affordable, pragmatic remediation partners.
- **Vendor flight from SMB.** The large global consultancies (Big 4, global MSSPs) price-out below the enterprise tier, leaving a vacuum in the SMB and lower mid-market served poorly or not at all.
- **Language and cultural proximity advantage.** UAE-based delivery with Arabic-ready materials and GCC cultural context competes favourably against offshore providers for engagement quality.

### 3.2 Total market context

The UAE cybersecurity services market is estimated in the low-to-mid single-digit billions of AED annually across all segments, with double-digit percentage year-on-year growth. Underwings does not need to capture a market share to succeed — our Year 1 revenue target of 400,000 AED represents a statistically invisible fraction of the market and requires only 8 to 12 successfully delivered engagements.

### 3.3 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Year 1 and Year 2

**Primary ICP — UAE mid-market (50 to 500 employees)**

- Industry: technology, professional services, healthcare / clinics, logistics, retail / e-commerce, fintech-adjacent SMEs.
- Trigger: upcoming audit, failed audit, data-breach scare, procurement obligation ("our enterprise client is asking us for ISO 27001 or PDPL compliance"), board-level directive, or tender requirement.
- Budget: 30,000 – 200,000 AED per engagement.
- Technical maturity: limited in-house security staff; typically one IT manager wearing the security hat.
- Buying authority: CEO, CFO, COO, or IT Director — not a dedicated CISO.

**Secondary ICP — UAE SMB (10 to 50 employees)**

- Industry: professional services, boutique consultancies, clinics, legal firms, real-estate brokerages, trading companies.
- Trigger: PDPL obligation, client-contract requirement, website or app incident.
- Budget: 10,000 – 50,000 AED per engagement.
- Entry point: Security Awareness Training, Phishing Simulation, or PDPL advisory.

**Tertiary ICP — UAE government-linked mid-size entities**

- Federal or emirate-level semi-government bodies, quasi-public enterprises, and their suppliers.
- Trigger: Dubai ISR v2 compliance, NESA / UAE IA V2 audit.
- Budget: 80,000 – 400,000 AED per engagement.
- Entry: via partnerships, referrals, and — from Year 2 — direct tender qualification once Underwings' own ISO 27001 certificate is issued.

### 3.4 Why mid-market and SMB is the right Year 1 focus

- Shorter sales cycle (weeks, not quarters).
- Fewer procurement hurdles (no pre-qualification beyond trade licence, professional indemnity, and VAT compliance).
- Lower collateral cost of competing (we do not need a 20-person proposal team).
- Referrals compound quickly in tight UAE SME networks.
- Pricing power is set by outcome, not procurement grid — which rewards our transparent-pricing model.

## 5. Positioning, Differentiation & Brand

### 5.1 Positioning statement

*For UAE mid-market and SMB companies facing a regulatory, audit, or security event, Underwings Cybersecurity Solutions delivers hands-on penetration testing, GRC implementation, human-risk programs, and curated security software — at transparent prices, with certified practitioners, and without the enterprise consultancy overhead.*

### 5.2 Differentiators

1. **Hands-on, not hand-wavy.** Every deliverable is built by a credentialed practitioner (CPTS / OSCP / ISO 27001 LA).
2. **Implementation, not advice.** We finish what we start — policies are written, controls are installed, staff are trained, pen tests end in remediated systems.
3. **Certification-body partnerships, not certification-body theatre.** We partner with accredited bodies instead of misrepresenting our scope.
4. **Transparent, published pricing frameworks.** Clients see price bands before a scoping call.
5. **UAE-based delivery with regional fluency.** NESA, ADHICS, PDPL, and Dubai ISR v2 are not bolt-ons — they are the Year-1 core.
6. **No inflated biography.** We say "launching in 2026" because we are, not "trusted since 2012" because we aren't.
7. **Compounding knowledge base.** Every engagement produces reusable playbooks and, from Year 1, published technical content at `underwings.org/blog` and `docs.underwings.org`.

### 5.3 Brand voice

Direct, technical, unpretentious. Plain English over jargon. Numbers over adjectives. "We found 12 critical issues and fixed 11 in the remediation phase" — not "we delivered a world-class engagement."
