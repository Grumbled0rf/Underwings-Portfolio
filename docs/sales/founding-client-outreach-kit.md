# Founding-Client Outreach Kit

> **Goal:** land Underwings' first 1–3 paying clients.
> **Reality this kit is built for:** zero delivered engagements, zero case
> studies, three genuinely credentialed principals, a UAE market that buys
> on trust and relationships.
> **The one thing to internalise:** a new firm's first clients come from
> *trust*, not marketing. Your first deals will come from people who already
> know one of you, or are one introduction away. Spend 80% of your energy
> on warm outreach, not cold.

---

## 1. The core problem and how we beat it

**The buyer's unspoken objection:** "You've never done this *as Underwings*. Why would I be your guinea pig?"

You beat it with four levers, used together:

1. **Named senior credentials, not logos.** You don't have client logos. You
   have something a 2-year-old agency often doesn't: the actual senior person
   does the work. Lead with the certs and the named-practitioner promise.
   - Manoj Prabhakaran — ISO 27001 Lead Auditor, CPTS, Azure Security
   - Nelson Durairaj — OSCP, CEH
   - Vinoth Samiyappa — CCNP, Fortinet NSE, Azure
2. **The Founding Client offer** (risk + price reversal — see §2).
3. **Regulatory urgency** where it exists — ADHICS v2 for Abu Dhabi
   healthcare is *mandatory*, with real deadlines. That's not a "nice to
   have" sale; it's a "you must" sale. Lead with it for healthcare targets.
4. **Proof-of-competence content** — when they Google you, they should find
   a genuinely useful ADHICS/ISO/PDPL article with your name on it. (That's
   the next deliverable after this kit.)

## 2. The Founding Client offer (the deal that lands deal #1)

Your website already advertises this — make it concrete and time-boxed.

**Offer:**
- **For the first 10 clients only.**
- **15–30% off** the standard engagement price (sliding scale — see below).
- **In exchange**, the client agrees to:
  - A written case study (anonymised if they prefer — "a UAE healthcare SaaS" works).
  - One reference call with a future prospect (after delivery).
  - A short LinkedIn testimonial / quote.

**Sliding discount — tie the discount to what you get back:**
| Client gives back | Discount |
|---|---|
| Named, public case study + logo + reference call | 30% |
| Anonymised case study + reference call | 20% |
| Anonymised case study only | 15% |

**The risk-reversal that closes nervous first buyers:**
Pick ONE of these and offer it explicitly — it's what overcomes "you're new":

- **Milestone payment:** 30% on signature, but the milestone payment is only
  due after they've seen the first deliverable (the gap assessment / draft
  findings) and are happy. They can walk before the big spend.
- **Gap-assessment-first:** do the initial gap assessment / scoping as a small
  paid piece (e.g. AED 5–10k). They judge your actual work before committing
  to the full engagement. Most convert; you've de-risked it for them.

> Pick milestone payment for project work (ISO, ADHICS, PDPL); pick
> gap-assessment-first for larger compliance builds where the buyer is most
> nervous.

## 3. Who to approach — the three tiers

Work top-down. Tier 1 converts at 10–30×, Tier 3 at 0.5–2%. Don't start cold.

### Tier 1 — Warm (do these THIS WEEK)
The people most likely to say yes are people who already trust one of you.
Each principal lists, by name:
- Former managers / colleagues now in a security, IT, compliance, or
  leadership role at any UAE company.
- Companies you previously worked at or consulted for (even pre-Underwings).
- 1st-degree LinkedIn connections in CISO / CIO / IT Manager / Compliance /
  Quality (healthcare) / Founder roles.
- Friends and family who run or work at an SMB.

**Action:** each principal commits 15 names to a shared sheet (§6). That's 45
warm targets. You need ~3 yeses. Entirely achievable from 45 warm contacts.

### Tier 2 — Referral (do in parallel)
You don't ask Tier 1 only "do you need this." You also ask:
> "Who do you know that's dealing with an ISO audit, an ADHICS deadline, or
> worried about a pen test this year?"

Warm intros from Tier 1 are your second-best source. One good intro > 100 cold emails.

### Tier 3 — Cold but qualified (only after Tier 1+2 are in motion)
Cold works *only* when the target has obvious, urgent need. Qualified cold lists:
- **Abu Dhabi healthcare entities** (DoH-licensed: hospitals, clinics, labs,
  day-care, telehealth) — ADHICS v2 is mandatory; deadline pressure is real.
  This is your single best cold segment because the *regulator* creates the urgency.
- UAE companies that **recently raised funding** (need security posture for
  enterprise sales / investor due diligence).
- Companies **hiring** for security/compliance roles (LinkedIn job posts =
  they know they have a gap).
- SaaS / fintech selling into regulated buyers (need pen-test reports + ISO to close deals).

## 4. Channels — UAE-specific

| Channel | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Warm (Tier 1/2) | UAE runs on WhatsApp. For anyone you know, this beats email. Voice notes work. |
| **LinkedIn DM** | Tier 1/2 2nd-degree, Tier 3 professional | Personalise. Reference something real about them. No pitch in message 1. |
| **Email** | Formal follow-up, sending the one-pager | After a WhatsApp/LinkedIn yes-to-chat, send the founding-client one-pager by email. |
| **In person** | Always best | GISEC, GITEX, DoH events, business councils (Indian Business Council, etc.), chamber events. UAE closes deals face-to-face. |
| **Referral / intro** | Highest conversion | Always ask "who else should I talk to?" |

**Do NOT** start a cold-email-at-scale machine yet. That's Phase H/I, gated on
warmup + tooling + your first paying client. Right now: hand-crafted, warm, few.

## 5. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "You're new / no track record." | "True — that's exactly why you get founding-client pricing and why the senior practitioner (not a junior) does your work personally. And we'll structure it so you see our first deliverable before the major payment is due. You're carrying very little risk." |
| "Too expensive." | "Compared to a failed ADHICS audit / a breach / losing an enterprise deal because you couldn't show a pen-test report — what's that worth? Founding pricing makes this the cheapest it will ever be with us." |
| "We already have a provider." | "Happy to give a second opinion on one specific area — say your ADHICS gap or a single web-app pen test. Low commitment, and you'll see how we work. ISO/NESA both require independent testing anyway." |
| "Not right now." | (Healthcare) "ADHICS isn't optional and the deadline won't move — the earlier we start, the calmer it is. Can I put a 20-min scoping call in for [date]?" / (Other) "No problem — can I check back in [60/90] days? In the meantime here's our ADHICS/ISO guide so you know how we think." |
| "Send me information." | "Will do — I'll email the founding-client one-pager now. Quick question so I send the right thing: is your priority compliance (ISO/ADHICS/PDPL), or security testing (pen test / VAPT)?" (Keep them talking; pure 'send info' = soft no, so re-engage.) |

## 6. Simple tracking (don't over-tool this)

A shared Google Sheet or a Krayin pipeline. Columns:

| Name | Company | Role | Principal | Tier | ICP | Channel | First contact | Status | Next action | Next date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Status values: `to contact` → `contacted` → `replied` → `call booked` →
`scoping` → `proposal sent` → `won` / `lost` / `nurture`.

**Better:** put each contacted lead into Krayin (Pipeline 4) with the right
source so it flows through your automation (scoring, brief, proposal). The
sheet is fine for the raw Tier-1 brainstorm; Krayin is the system of record
once you've made contact.
