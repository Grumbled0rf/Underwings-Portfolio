## E. The 4 rules

1. **Personalise line 1.** If line 1 could be sent to anyone, it gets ignored.
2. **Message 1 asks for a conversation, not a sale.** No attachments, no price
   in the first message (except the soft founding-client mention).
3. **Always end with one clear ask** — a question or a specific next step.
4. **Follow up once** after ~3 days if no reply. Then leave it (move to nurture).
   One good follow-up doubles reply rates; a third makes you a pest.
